# 🍽️ DineEase

DineEase is a modern **restaurant order management system** that helps restaurants handle dine-in, takeaway, and online orders in one simple platform.

It is designed to make restaurant operations **faster, easier, and more organized** for both staff and customers.

---

## ✨ What DineEase Does

DineEase brings everything a restaurant needs into one system:

- 📋 Digital menu browsing
- 🪑 Table-based dine-in ordering
- 🧾 Order management for staff
- 👨‍🍳 Kitchen order display system
- 📊 Basic management dashboard
- 💳 Smooth order processing flow

---

## 👥 Who It Is For

- 🧑‍🍳 Restaurant staff (waiters, cashiers, kitchen team)
- 🧑‍💼 Restaurant managers
- 🍔 Customers dining in or ordering food

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 For Customers
- Browse digital menu
- Customize food orders
- Place orders easily

### 👨‍🍳 For Kitchen Staff
- View live incoming orders
- Update order status (Preparing / Ready / Served)
- Reduce confusion in busy hours

### 👨‍💼 For Management & Staff
- Manage tables and orders
- Add or update menu items
- Manage staff accounts
- Track restaurant operations

---

## ⚡ Why DineEase?

Traditional restaurant systems often rely on paper orders or disconnected tools, which can lead to:
- Order mistakes
- Slow service
- Poor coordination

DineEase solves this by:
- Centralizing all orders in one system
- Improving communication between staff and kitchen
- Reducing human errors
- Speeding up service time
